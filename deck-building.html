Deck Builders & Restoration in the Delta Area

A great deck is one of the best additions to a BC home — but our coastal climate is hard on outdoor wood. Texora Painting builds new decks and brings tired, weathered decks back to life across Tsawwassen, Ladner, and Delta. Whether you want a fresh build, structural repairs, or a full sand-and-refinish, we treat every deck like it's our own backyard.

We handle the full range: new deck construction, replacing rotten or split boards, rebuilding railings and stairs, sanding down grey weathered wood, and finishing with premium stains and sealers built for the Lower Mainland's wet winters. If your deck just needs a fresh coat, we also offer dedicated deck staining & refinishing.

Every deck project starts with a free on-site assessment and a clear written quote — no obligation, no surprises.

Deck Building & Restoration — FAQs

Both. We build brand-new decks, rebuild and repair existing structures (boards, railings, stairs), and refinish weathered decks with sanding, staining, and sealing. Tell us what you need during your free quote.

It varies widely — a sand-and-restain typically runs a few hundred to a couple thousand dollars, while new construction or a full rebuild is quoted based on size, materials, and design. We provide a detailed free written quote after seeing the deck.

Deck building and restoration covers the structure — construction, board and railing replacement, and repairs. Deck staining is the finishing step — sanding and applying protective stain or sealer. We offer both, and many decks need a bit of each.

Spring through early fall is ideal, when the wood is dry and temperatures suit staining and sealing. We book up in peak season, so reach out early to lock in your spot.
