Deck Builders & Restoration in the Delta Area

A great deck is one of the best additions to a BC home, but our coastal climate is hard on outdoor wood. Texora Painting & Drywall builds new decks and brings tired, weathered decks back to life across Tsawwassen, Ladner, and Delta. Whether you want a fresh build, structural repairs, or a full sand-and-refinish, we treat every deck like it's our own backyard.

We handle the full range: new deck construction, replacing rotten or split boards, rebuilding railings and stairs, sanding down grey weathered wood, and finishing with premium stains and sealers built for the Lower Mainland's wet winters. If your deck just needs a fresh coat, we also offer dedicated deck staining & refinishing.

Every deck project starts with a free on-site assessment and a clear written quote, no obligation, no surprises.
