Exterior paint problems we see in Port Moody

  • Moss, algae and mildew on wood siding and cedar shingles, from heavy rain, fog off the Inlet and little sun under the tree canopy.
  • Caulking that has cracked or pulled away at window and door trim, which lets Port Moody's heavy rain in at the joints.
  • Mixed siding: HardiePlank on newer Heritage Mountain homes, wood lap siding in Pleasantside, stucco near Old Orchard and cedar shingles along Murray Street.

Caulking and joint sealing

A lot of exterior repaint failures start at the joints: window and door trim, corner boards and anywhere two materials meet let water in once caulking cracks. Ask whether a quote includes removing failing caulk before resealing, and whether the sealant stays flexible through temperature swings.

Dry times and cure windows

Published dry and recoat times assume close to ideal temperature and humidity, and cooler, damper weather slows both. Recoating before the first coat has cured can trap solvent that shows up months later as blistering, so ask how the schedule flexes in wet weather.

What to ask for in an exterior quote

  • Whether moss and mildew treatment is included or billed separately if growth is heavier than expected once washing starts
  • Whether failing caulk is dug out and replaced, not just painted over, and what sealant product is proposed
