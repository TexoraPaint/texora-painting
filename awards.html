BBB Accredited Business since 29 September 2026

We have been BBB Accredited since 29 September 2026 and hold an A- rating. Our BBB profile lists the business as a general partnership in Delta, BC.

To be accredited, a business affirms that it meets BBB's eight Standards for Trust and has to keep meeting them while it holds accreditation:

  • Build trust
  • Advertise honestly
  • Tell the truth
  • Be transparent
  • Honor promises
  • Be responsive
  • Safeguard privacy
  • Embody integrity
