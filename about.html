Local Roots

Painters who actually know Tsawwassen & Delta

We're based right on 56th Street in Tsawwassen — the peninsula tucked between the Strait of Georgia and Boundary Bay — so the homes we paint are our neighbours' homes. From the ocean-view properties of English Bluff and the character houses around Boundary Bay and Beach Grove to Pebble Hill, Tsawwassen Heights, The Terrace and the newer Southlands — and just north into Ladner and greater Delta — we know how homes here are built, weathered, and best cared for.

Living this close to the water has its perks and its quirks — and protecting homes from it is exactly what we specialise in. Tsawwassen enjoys the driest, sunniest microclimate in Metro Vancouver — nearly 40% less rain than downtown Vancouver — but the salt air blowing in off the Strait of Georgia and Boundary Bay is a very different challenge than most Lower Mainland cities face. That marine salt, paired with strong coastal sun, breaks ordinary paint down faster, corrodes fasteners, and works its way into trim and fascia boards where rot hides behind still-fresh-looking paint.

We've learned exactly how coastal homes fail here — and how to stop it. We wash off salt build-up, repair and prime bare wood, replace rotten fascia, seal end-grain, and specify premium, salt- and UV-resistant coatings chosen for seaside exposure. It's the kind of detail an out-of-town crew simply won't think to do — and it's why Tsawwassen and Boundary Bay homeowners trust us with their exteriors.

Because we live and work here, we show up on time, protect your property, respect your street and your neighbours, and stand behind the work long after the day's ferry traffic on Highway 17 has cleared. Hire Texora and you're hiring the painters from down the road — not a crew shipped in from across the Lower Mainland.
