What Affects Your Final Price

Prep & repairs — the single biggest variable. Patching, sanding, and priming take time but are what make a finish last. Size & ceiling height — more surface and taller walls mean more paint and labour. Colour & coats — big colour changes and premium finishes need extra coats. Access & condition — occupied homes, delicate surfaces, and hard-to-reach exteriors add time.
