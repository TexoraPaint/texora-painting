How a drywall repair job runs in Port Moody

1

Assessment and quote

We inspect the damage, check for underlying moisture or structural causes, and provide a written quote that covers repair, priming, and paint touch-up.

2

Repair and reinforcement

We cut out damaged sections, install backing or new drywall panels, tape all joints with fibreglass mesh, and apply multiple coats of joint compound.

Once cured, the repair is sanded smooth and any existing wall texture (orange peel, skip trowel, or smooth) is replicated to blend with the surrounding surface.

Keeping a lived-in home clean during a repair

Before booking a repair bigger than a small patch, ask exactly how dust gets contained, not only how it gets cleaned up. Plastic sheeting over doorways and a vacuum fitted for fine dust are the usual answer.

Corners, trim and outlets

Outside corners need rigid corner bead sitting straight before any mud goes on, or the corner reads as a hump under paint. Cuts around window and door trim run clean to the casing, and outlet and switch boxes sit flush so the cover plates seat.

Cracks that come back after painting

A crack that reopens within a year usually means movement, from a house settling or seasonal soil shifts, rather than a bad first repair. Fibreglass mesh tape flexes more than paper tape, which is why it goes on every joint here.

Between the last coat and the paint

Compound that feels set after a few hours can still be soft underneath, and priming it too soon traps moisture that shows later as a soft spot or uneven sheen. Ask how a contractor judges a patch is ready rather than assuming a fixed number of hours.

If you're in a strata unit

Drywall repair involves cutting, sanding, and running a vacuum, none of it quiet. If you're in a Port Moody condo or townhouse complex, check your strata's bylaws for approved work hours before booking anything beyond a small patch.

What to ask for in a repair quote

  • Will the crew match the existing wall texture, and what texture is currently on the wall?
  • Is the cause of the damage, leak, settling, or impact, being addressed, or just the surface patched?
