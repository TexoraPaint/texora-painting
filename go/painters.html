How it works

From free quote to final walkthrough

  1. 1Free on-site quote.We look at the job and give you a written price. Most quotes are delivered the same or next day.
  2. 2Colour consultation.We bring swatches and help you choose colours and sheens.
  3. 3Prep.Surfaces washed, sanded, patched and primed. This is where a paint job lasts or fails.
  4. 4Protection.Floors, furniture and fixtures covered before a can is opened.
  5. 5Paint.Brush, roller or spray, whichever suits the surface.
  6. 6Walkthrough.We walk the job with you and fix anything we missed before we call it done.

A single room usually takes one full day, a whole-home interior 3 to 7 days, and a house exterior 3 to 6 days.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to paint a house?
A single room is usually $300 to $600, and a full 2,000 to 2,500 sq ft interior $6,000 to $14,000. A standard house exterior runs $5,000 to $12,000. The written quote after the visit is your price.
Is the quote really free?
Yes. It is free, in person and in writing, with no obligation.
Who comes out to quote?
One of the owners. Texora is owned and run by two local partners who meet every homeowner, and our uniformed crews do the work.
What warranty do I get?
A written workmanship warranty. The period and what it covers depend on the job, and they are written into your estimate before work starts.
What paint do you use?
Benjamin Moore, Sherwin-Williams and Dulux premium lines. We can also use paint you have already bought.
Do I need to move my furniture?
Move small items and valuables. We move the large furniture to the middle of the room and cover everything.
Where do you work?
Delta, including Tsawwassen, Ladner and North Delta, plus Richmond, Surrey and White Rock. Residential only.
