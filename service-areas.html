Towns we paint in

Ladner

Interior and exterior painting for Ladner homes, including heritage properties. Lath-and-plaster walls in the 1930s houses around Ladner Village need different patching than drywall.

Painters in Ladner →

Tsawwassen

Interior, exterior and cabinet painting in Tsawwassen, a primary service area for us. Salt air, high humidity and wind-driven rain are hardest on homes facing the water.

Painters in Tsawwassen →

Delta

Interior and exterior painting across Delta: North Delta, Ladner, Tsawwassen and Sunshine Hills. A wet season from October through April narrows the window for exterior work.

Painters in Delta →

Coquitlam

Walls, ceilings, trim and doors for Coquitlam homes and condos. Housing runs from newer builds on Burke Mountain to heritage properties in Maillardville.

Painters in Coquitlam →

Port Coquitlam

Interior and exterior painting, drywall repair and installation in Port Coquitlam. Stucco is very common on the 1980s and 90s homes, and it needs different prep than wood or Hardie board.

Painters in Port Coquitlam →

Port Moody

Interior and exterior painting, drywall repair and installation in Port Moody. Heavy rain, fog off the Inlet and the tree canopy bring moss, algae and mildew to wood siding and cedar shingles.

Painters in Port Moody →

Richmond

Interior, exterior and cabinet painting for Richmond homes, condos and townhouses. The high groundwater table keeps water close to the surface, so bubbling or staining near the bottom of an exterior wall gets a look before painting.

Painters in Richmond →

Surrey

Interior, exterior and cabinet painting across Surrey, South Surrey, Fleetwood and Cloverdale. Builder-grade flat paint on newer homes usually needs priming first, since it can flash or absorb unevenly.

Painters in Surrey →

Burnaby

Interior, exterior and cabinet painting for Burnaby homes, condos and townhouses. Higher ground toward Burnaby Mountain gets more winter snow than Vancouver or Richmond.

Painters in Burnaby →

New Westminster

Interior and exterior painting for New Westminster homes, including heritage properties. Plaster cracks in the older homes follow the lath and come back if they are taped over like a drywall seam.

Painters in New Westminster →

North Vancouver

Interior and exterior painting for North Vancouver homes, including heritage and custom properties. North-facing walls in the forested, wet setting grow moss and algae and stay damp long after a storm.

Painters in North Vancouver →

Vancouver

Interior, exterior and cabinet painting and drywall repair across Vancouver. Wet winters test exterior coatings, with more rain downtown than at the airport.

Painters in Vancouver →
