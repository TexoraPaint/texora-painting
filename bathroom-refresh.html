Refresh Your Bathroom — Paint, Drywall & Vanity

Not every bathroom needs a full gut-renovation. Often a fresh, professional paint job, some drywall repair, and a refinished vanity completely transform the space — for a fraction of the cost and disruption. That's exactly what Texora Painting specializes in for homeowners across Tsawwassen, Ladner, and Delta.

Bathrooms are humid, so they need the right approach: mould-resistant, moisture-tolerant paints, proper prep, and repair of any water-damaged drywall before finishing. We also refinish vanities and cabinets with a durable sprayed finish — see our cabinet painting service.

Please note: we focus on the paint, drywall, and cabinet side of bathroom updates — we don't do plumbing or tile work. If that's all you need to refresh your space, we're a perfect, cost-effective fit. Free quotes, always.
