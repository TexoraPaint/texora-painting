What goes into a clean trim and door paint job?

Baseboards and casings are sanded, filled at cracks and nail holes, caulked at the wall joint, and cut in by hand rather than relying on tape for the line where trim meets wall. Doors are ideally removed and painted flat, since gravity pulls brush marks and drips downward on a door left standing.

Ask whether hinges, handles and knobs come off before painting starts, because painting around hardware leaves a rough edge once it's back on.

A flat sheen that hides small flaws on a wall shows every scuff and fingerprint on a baseboard or door, so trim and doors usually get semi-gloss, which is washable and holds up to daily bumps.

Do your trim, doors or ceilings need repainting?

  • Trim or baseboards that have yellowed or chipped.
  • Ceiling stains, which need a stain-blocking primer before the topcoat.
  • Doors you want in a new colour or a different sheen.

Trim, door and ceiling work we do in Delta

Trim and baseboards

Baseboards, door casings, window casings, and mouldings are lightly sanded, filled at cracks and nail holes, caulked at the wall joint, and painted in a semi-gloss or satin finish. Clean lines where trim meets wall are cut by hand, with no bleed or overlap.

Interior doors

Doors are ideally removed and painted flat for the best finish. Where this isn't practical, we mask carefully and brush the panel sections in sequence. We use a semi-gloss or gloss finish for durability and washability.

Ceilings

Ceilings are rolled with a flat or matte finish to hide imperfections and diffuse light evenly. We cut in at the ceiling-wall junction by hand for a straight line. If your ceiling has staining, we apply a stain-blocking primer before topcoat.

Feature and accent ceilings

We paint accent ceilings in a contrasting colour or a deep hue, and we'll help you choose the right colour and finish.

More about trim, door and ceiling painting in Delta

How soon can freshly painted doors be closed?

Ask the crew which doors are ready for everyday use when they leave, and which still need another day. A door that feels dry after a few hours can still tack against a strike plate or hinge and peel later if it's closed too soon.

How do you get a flat, even ceiling finish?

Cutting in the ceiling-wall line by hand matters as much as the rolling, because a ceiling shows every roller lap once light hits it sideways. Stains from an old roof leak or a bathroom fan need a stain-blocking primer first, or they bleed through the topcoat.

What to decide before a trim and door painting quote

  • Baseboards and casings only, or the full trim package
  • How many interior doors are included
  • Whether exterior doors are part of the scope
  • Sheen preference for trim versus doors
  • Accent ceiling colour, if you want one

What does the trim, door and ceiling warranty cover?

Trim, door, and ceiling work is covered by the same written workmanship warranty as any other job, one to three years depending on scope, stated on your estimate. That covers paint that fails from how it was prepped or applied, and any carpentry we installed that comes loose, like a piece of crown moulding. It doesn't cover a door slammed hard enough to chip the edge, or wear from daily use over the years.

There's a final walkthrough on every project too, so you can flag anything in the agreed scope before we call it finished.
